Hello, My name is Pritam Bhat, a 44-year-old resident of Pune, Maharashtra. I have been diagnosed with Oral Cancer and am currently undergoing treatment at Apollo Hospital, Navi Mumbai under the guidance of Dr. Jyoti Bajpai.

Although I have undergone multiple cycles of chemotherapy, completed radiation therapy and immunotherapy, my treatment is still ongoing.
